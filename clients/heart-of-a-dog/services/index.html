Private lesson packages

Private lessons are designed for owners who want to take an active role in their dog's training. Each session provides hands-on guidance, practical exercises and personalized instruction based on your dog's individual needs and your lifestyle.

Lessons are typically recommended weekly or biweekly to encourage consistency and allow time to practice between sessions.

Structured Training in a Real Home Environment

Board & train programs

Our board and train programs provide an immersive training experience where your dog stays in a home environment while receiving consistent training, structure and daily guidance.

Programs are designed to develop foundational skills, improve everyday manners, build confidence and address individual training goals. Each program includes training equipment, private lessons for owner education, and regular training updates shared through Instagram.

2-Week Board & Train
$1,550

14 days · Foundational Skills

Ideal for dogs needing an introduction to structure, communication and basic training.

Training may include:

  • Basic obedience commands
  • Introduction to leash manners
  • Place training
  • Crate training and settling
  • Household boundaries
  • Introduction to impulse control
  • Age-appropriate puppy skills
Includes: training equipment and 2 private lessons
3-Week Board & Train
