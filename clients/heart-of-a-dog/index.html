Meet Your Trainer

Hi, I'm Jaila.

As the owner of three dogs, I know how hectic life can be, and how much easier everything gets with a trained dog. I've been training for more than five years as a balanced trainer, using the methods that work best for you and your dog.

I specialize in basic obedience and household behaviors: potty training, crate training, walking on a leash and more. My goal is for you to enjoy a walk in the park or dinner on a patio without worrying about how your dog will behave.

Questions

Frequently asked

What is a board and train program?

Your dog stays with Jaila for 2, 3 or 4 weeks and trains every day in a real home setting. When your dog comes home, you get private lessons so you know exactly how to keep the results going.

What will my dog learn?

Every program covers household manners and core obedience: sit, down, stay, leave it, heel, no, and place. Longer programs add an automatic sit, precision heel, commands on a 15-foot leash and more socialization.

Do you offer private lessons instead of board and train?

Yes. Private lessons are six one-on-one sessions with you and your dog, including training equipment. Virtual lessons are available if distance is a concern.

Do you offer dog boarding?

Yes. Boarding is $60 a night and includes pick-up and drop-off. Your dog gets plenty of love and attention while you're away.

How do I get started?

Call, text or email Jaila. Share your dog's name, age and breed and what you'd like to work on, and she'll recommend the right program.
