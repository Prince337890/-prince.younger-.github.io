Questions

Frequently asked

Which program is right for my dog?

It depends on your dog and your goals. Private lessons suit owners who want to take an active role, board and train gives your dog an immersive training experience, and virtual lessons work well for puppy training and household routines. A single lesson or evaluation ($125) is a good way to talk through your goals and choose the best approach.

What is a board and train program?

Your dog stays in a home environment for 2, 3 or 4 weeks and receives consistent training, structure and daily guidance. Every program includes training equipment, private lessons to teach you how to keep the progress going, and regular training updates shared on Instagram.

Are payment plans available?

Yes. Payment plans are available for board and train programs and private lesson packages.

Where do you offer training?

Heart of a Dog Training is based in Dallas, GA and serves Dallas, Hiram, Powder Springs, Douglasville, Marietta and surrounding Metro Atlanta communities. Private lessons can take place in your home or an appropriate public training environment. Additional mileage fees apply beyond 15 miles, and travel costs are discussed before scheduling.

Do you offer dog boarding?

Yes. Overnight boarding is $65 a night in a structured home environment. Pickup and drop-off are available for $50 each, arranged in advance and subject to availability.
