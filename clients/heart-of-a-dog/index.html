Questions

Frequently asked

Do you offer in-home dog training?

Yes. Most sessions take place in your home and neighborhood, where your dog's habits actually happen. We serve Powder Springs, Marietta and the surrounding West Cobb and Metro Atlanta area.

How old should my puppy be to start training?

Puppies can start as soon as they come home, usually around 8 to 10 weeks. Early training prevents problem habits like biting, jumping and potty accidents.

Can you help with a reactive or aggressive dog?

Yes. Behavior modification is a core part of our work. We start with a consultation to understand your dog's triggers, then build a structured plan you can follow between sessions.

How do I get started?

Call, text or email to book a consultation. We'll talk through your goals and recommend the right program for your dog.
