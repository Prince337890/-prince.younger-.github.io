Puppy Foundations

The first few months shape your dog for life. We build good habits early so problem behaviors never take hold.

  • Potty and crate training
  • Nipping, mouthing and bite inhibition
  • Safe socialization with people, dogs and new places
  • Name recognition, sit, down and come
  • Early leash skills and handling

Obedience & Manners
