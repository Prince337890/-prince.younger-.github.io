Meet Your Trainer

Helping dogs and their owners better understand one another.

Owner & Trainer

Hi, I'm Jaila!

I'm the owner and trainer behind Heart of a Dog Training. With approximately seven years of hands-on dog training experience and a background in professional dog training education, I'm passionate about helping dogs and their owners better understand one another.

My approach combines structure, clear communication, consistency, and an understanding of each dog's individual personality and needs. I believe training should go beyond teaching commands. It should help dogs develop confidence, learn appropriate behaviors, and successfully navigate everyday life.

One of the most important parts of my job is educating owners. I want you to understand not only what to do with your dog, but why you're doing it and how to continue making progress long after our sessions end.

Whether we're working through puppy behaviors, improving leash manners, or addressing more challenging behaviors, my goal is to help create a calmer, more confident dog and a stronger relationship between you and your companion.
