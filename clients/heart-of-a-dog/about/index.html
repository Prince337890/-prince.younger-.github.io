Meet Jaila

Helping dogs and their owners understand each other better.

Founder & Trainer

Why I became a dog trainer

As the owner of three dogs, I know how hectic life can be. A trained dog makes everything easier, and I want every owner to have that.

I've been training for more than five years, and in that time I've become a balanced trainer. That lets me choose the methods that work best for you and your dog.

I specialize in basic obedience and household behaviors, covering potty training, crate training, walking on a leash and more. I also offer in-home board and train programs, so I can personally shape your dog's habits to fit the lifestyle you want.
